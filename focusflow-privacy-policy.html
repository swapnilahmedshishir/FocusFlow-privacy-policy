Privacy Policy

FocusFlow: Focus Timer & Streak
Effective date: November 5, 2026

XeTriva Software ("we", "our", or "us") provides FocusFlow: Focus Timer & Streak (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information may be handled when you use the App and how it is used, shared, and protected.

In short: Focus sessions and streak progress are designed to be stored locally on your device. The App does not require you to create an account to use its core focus-timer features.

1. Information We Handle

Information stored on your device. The App may store your focus-session history, timer preferences, streak information, earned rewards, and theme settings locally on your device so that its features work and your progress can be displayed.

Advertising information. The App may display rewarded advertisements. When ads are enabled, Google AdMob and/or its mediation or advertising partners may collect or receive information such as advertising identifiers, IP address, device and app information, approximate location derived from IP address, and ad interaction data. The information collected depends on the advertising providers, your device settings, and applicable consent choices.

Diagnostics and technical information. The App or its third-party service providers may process limited technical information, such as app version, device model, operating-system version, crash information, and performance data, to maintain and improve reliability. The exact information depends on the SDKs enabled in the released version.

Purchases. If the App offers paid features or subscriptions, payment transactions are handled by the applicable app store. We do not receive or store your full payment-card details. The app store and any purchase-management provider integrated into the App may process purchase identifiers, subscription status, and transaction-related information to validate and manage purchases.

2. How We Use Information

  • To run focus timers, breaks, streaks, rewards, themes, and related App features.
  • To display and measure rewarded advertisements, where enabled.
  • To validate purchases and provide paid features, where offered.
  • To diagnose crashes, improve performance, prevent abuse, and maintain App security.
  • To comply with legal obligations and respond to legitimate requests.

3. Notifications and Background Timer

FocusFlow may use Android notifications and a foreground service to keep an active countdown visible and running while the App is in the background. These functions are used to provide the timer experience. You can manage notification permissions and settings through your Android device settings. Disabling permissions or restricting background activity may affect these features.

4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell your personal information. We may allow service providers to process information on our behalf where needed to provide the App, including advertising, app-store billing, purchase validation, and crash or performance diagnostics.

Third-party providers process information under their own privacy policies and terms. Depending on the services enabled in the App, these may include:

  • Google Privacy Policy — Google services, including AdMob advertising where used.
  • Google User Consent Policy — information about consent requirements for applicable Google services.
  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy — purchase and subscription management, if integrated into the App.
  • Amazon Privacy Notice — if you obtain or use an Amazon Appstore version of the App.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Focus-session records, preferences, and progress stored locally remain on your device until they are deleted by the App, you clear the App's storage, or you uninstall the App. Uninstalling the App or clearing its storage may permanently remove locally stored progress.

Information handled by third-party providers is retained according to their own retention policies. Please refer to the relevant provider's privacy policy for details and available deletion or advertising-privacy controls.

6. Your Choices and Controls

  • Use Android settings to manage notification permissions.
  • Use your device's advertising settings to reset or limit use of the advertising ID, where supported.
  • Use available consent or privacy controls presented by the advertising provider, where applicable.
  • Clear the App's storage or uninstall the App to remove locally stored App data.
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions through the app store where the purchase was made.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information to us through a third-party service used by the App, please contact us so we can review the concern. Advertising providers may apply their own age-related restrictions and safeguards.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the App. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You should keep your device and operating system updated and use the security controls available on your device.

9. International Data Processing

Third-party service providers may process information on servers located in countries other than your own. Where this occurs, those providers are responsible for applying the safeguards required by applicable law.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, our services, or applicable legal requirements change. We will publish the updated version at this page and revise the effective date above. Your continued use of the App after an update means you have had the opportunity to review the revised policy.
